## Brand & Style
The design system channels high-end Sri Lankan textile craftsmanship into a premier digital editorial showcase. Marrying historic batik wax-resist artistry with modern haute couture sensibilities, the experience evokes exclusivity, cultural pride, and tactile luxury. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an opulent, dark-mode-first editorial style, balancing deep, rich jewel tones with delicate antique gold filigree and watermark mandala textures. Rather than functioning as a standard utilitarian store, the interface functions as a salon and digital lookbook. Visual elements celebrate slow fashion and bespoke craftsmanship, substituting friction-heavy transactional checkout funnels with direct, white-glove bespoke inquiry and WhatsApp concierge order pathways.

## Colors
The palette draws direct inspiration from traditional natural dye vats and ceremonial Sri Lankan hand-painted textiles. 

- **Primary (`#C49A52` - Antique Gold):** Used for key focal points, interactive highlights, delicate ornamental borders, and bespoke calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#581B24` - Dark Burgundy):** Acts as the foundational ambient depth and card surface tone, complemented by its deep shadow counterpart Rich Maroon (`#3B1018`).
- **Tertiary (`#182A4A` - Deep Indigo):** Imparts ancestral dye contrast, applied to backdrop gradients, structural header frames, and high-fashion badges.
- **Neutral (`#F6EFE3` - Warm Cream):** Provides luminous contrast against deep backgrounds for primary typography and crisp iconography, softened down to Soft Beige (`#E8D8C3`) for supporting copy.
- **Accents:** Emerald Green (`#285C45`) serves verification states and WhatsApp concierge action triggers, while Burnt Orange (`#B85C32`) provides rich warmth for limited-run garment badges.

## Typography
Typographic scale reflects the dramatic rhythm of high-end print lookbooks. Headings set in Playfair Display bring editorial elegance and traditional grace, nodding to classical luxury with italic accents for collection taglines. Body text relies on Plus Jakarta Sans, maintaining clear legibility across dark, layered backgrounds.

All uppercase label styles must incorporate wide letter spacing (`letterSpacing >= 0.08em`) to mirror archival museum and gallery placard formatting.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column editorial grid for desktop screens (reducing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile). Generous negative space and asymmetric column spans mimic physical magazine spreads.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px to 64px outer margins. Garments and editorial imagery utilize alternating 5-column, 7-column, and full-span configurations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Two-column grid configurations for catalog pieces.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Editorial images take full bleed with vertical typographic lockups.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy is constructed through tonal layering and delicate antique gold borders rather than heavy standard drop shadows.

- **Base Canvas:** Deepest shade `#140608` or `#0e1829`, layered with subtle SVG watermark batik mandala textures set to 4-6% opacity.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Panels):** Dark Burgundy (`#3B1018`) with a 1px perimeter outline of Antique Gold at 25% opacity (`rgba(196, 154, 82, 0.25)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals & Flyouts):** Frosted Deep Indigo (`rgba(24, 42, 74, 0.9)`) combined with a 16px backdrop blur and a fine 1px gold border (`rgba(196, 154, 82, 0.45)`).
- **Ambient Glow:** High-priority focus items emit an ultra-diffused, 24px blur golden ambient halo (`rgba(196, 154, 82, 0.12)`) in place of harsh dark drop shadows.

## Shapes
A disciplined, soft geometry (0.25rem / 4px base radius) is paired with circular motif accents. This low-radius architectural form provides sharp structure to product cards and editorial viewports, preserving a couture poise. 

Circular framing (100% radius) is reserved strictly for artisanal avatars, monogram seals, fabric zoom viewports, and floating WhatsApp concierge buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Concierge & WhatsApp Ordering):** Antique Gold (`#C49A52`) fill with Deep Maroon (`#3B1018`) bold text, uppercase styling, soft roundedness (`0.25rem`), and an optional embedded WhatsApp glyph. Hover transitions trigger an elevated ambient gold glow.
- **Secondary (Editorial Story & Lookbook):** Transparent fill with a 1px Antique Gold border (`#C49A52`) and Warm Cream text. Inverts on hover to an antique gold tint with 15% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Text Actions:** Warm Cream with an animated bottom underline rendered in antique gold.

### Product & Story Cards
- Deep Burgundy card base framed in a 1px antique gold filigree stroke.
- Image aspect ratios prioritize tall vertical orientations (3:4 or 4:5) to showcase full-length batik drape, sari falls, and motif details.
- Subtle inner corner ornamental motifs or micro-corner accents on premium archive garments.

### Chips & Badges
- **Collection Badges:** Pill-shaped or subtle rounded tags with a Deep Indigo base, Antique Gold border, and uppercase gold metadata typography.
- **Fabric/Craft Badges (e.g., 'Pure Silk', 'Hand-Waxed'):** Low-contrast terracotta or muted gold fill with soft cream text.

### Direct Order Concierge Modal
- Eliminates standard cart/checkout flows.
- Displays pre-filled garment code, fabric batch number, sizing customizer, and an immediate "Inquire via WhatsApp" primary action that deep-links directly to a concierge representative.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark Maroon surface (`#3B1018`) with Warm Cream placeholder text and a bottom border in Antique Gold (`rgba(196, 154, 82, 0.4)`).
- Focus states illuminate the border to full `#C49A52` accompanied by a soft golden underline bloom.

### Ornamental Separators & Motifs
- Section breaks feature centered miniature batik circular floral or mandala vector icons flanked by 1px tapering gold filigree rule lines.
