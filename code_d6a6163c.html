Living Sri Lankan Heritage

A Tapestry of Heritage & Contemporary Silhouette

“We do not merely apply dye to cloth. With boiling beeswax, hand-forged canting pens, and generational instinct, we craft wearable heirloom poetry honoring the resplendent soul of Sri Lanka.”

Handcrafted with devotion in Chilaw, Madampe, Sri Lanka, Chamathkara Bathik transforms centuries of island textile traditions into couture gowns, classic drapes, and distinguished evening wear. Every crackle in the wax tells a story of human touch and natural elements.

The Intricate Alchemy of Wax, Fire & Natural Water

Unlike machine-printed textiles, every square inch of Chamathkara Batik bears witness to an intensive multi-week ritual. The master artisan applies molten paraffin and bees' wax freehand using copper spouts, reserving untouched threads before immersion into color vats.

01 draw
Canting Freehand

Intricate lines sketched with boiling wax onto 100% natural fibers.

02 palette
Multi-Bath Dyeing

Graduated cold immersion baths creating singular depth and tone.

03 grain
Wax Fracturing

Intentional cracking under pressure yielding iconic batik crackle veining.

04 water_drop
Boiling & Release

Dewaxed in fresh boiling water, unmasking clean silk beneath.
